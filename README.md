## 1. 内容分级 / Content Rating

**成人内容警告 / 18+ Only**

本宇宙中的部分作品包含成人内容，包括但不限于：

- 强烈的性暗示和明确的性内容描写
- 暴力和战斗场景
- 复杂的道德困境和黑暗主题

**所有虚构内容均为艺术创作，不鼓励在现实中进行类似行为。**

---

**Adult Content Warning / 18+ Only**

Some works in this universe contain adult content, including but not limited to:

- Strong sexual implications and explicit sexual content descriptions
- Violence and combat scenes
- Complex moral dilemmas and dark themes

**All fictional content is artistic creation and does not encourage similar behavior in reality.**

## 3. 关于本企划 / About This Project

**Beastkin Universe** 是一个多元兽人题材创作宇宙，包含多个独立且互相关联的世界观，由社区驱动的开放式创作平台。

我们的核心理念是「同一个概念，多重演绎」。每个世界观都从独特的视角探索兽人题材，为创作者和读者提供丰富的体验。

---

**Beastkin Universe** is a multifaceted furry-themed creative universe containing multiple
independent yet interconnected worlds, a community-driven open creative platform.

Our core philosophy is "One Concept, Multiple Interpretations." Each world explores furry themes
from unique perspectives, offering rich experiences for both creators and readers.

---

## 4. 世界观概览 / World Overview

### 4.1 🛡️ Beastshield (兽盾)

**黑暗企业帝国**
一个由兽盾公司掌控的世界，这家庞大的企业帝国控制着社会的方方面面。在这里，兽人员工们经营着从军事安保到娱乐产业的所有领域，形成了一个权力无边、规则自定的组织。

---

**Dark Corporate Empire**
A world controlled by the Beast Shield Corporation, a massive corporate empire dominating every
aspect of society. Here, beastkin employees manage everything from military security to
entertainment, forming an organization with boundless power and its own rules.

---

### 4.2 🔥 Beastshield Reforged (兽盾新纪元)

**末日重生**
在文明崩溃后的废墟上，兽盾公司以全新的形态重生。这是一个关于生存、权力重组和人性考验的世界，兽人们在废土上建立新的秩序。

---

**Post-Apocalyptic Rebirth**
In the ruins of collapsed civilization, the Beast Shield Corporation is reborn in a new form. This
is a world of survival, power restructuring, and tests of humanity, where beastkin establish new
orders in the wasteland.

---

### 4.3 🤝 United Beasts Alliance (万兽盟)

**跨种族同盟**
多个兽人种族放下分歧，组成联合联盟对抗共同的威胁。这是一个关于合作、外交和跨文化理解的世界，探索不同兽人种族如何共存共荣。

---

**Cross-Species Alliance**
Multiple beastkin races set aside differences to form a united alliance against common threats. This
world explores cooperation, diplomacy, and cross-cultural understanding among different beastkin
species.

---

## 5. 创作路径推荐 / Recommended Creation Path

**新创作者建议从改编作品开始**：

- 在 `adaptation-works/` 目录下开始创作
- 使用改编模板（性质字段为 `a`）
- 获得社区反馈，逐步完善作品
- 作品完成后可申请晋升为官方作品

这是融入社区、理解世界观最顺畅的路径。详细指南请参阅：[通用故事模板指南](docs/universal-story-template-guide-chinese.md)

---

**New creators are recommended to start with adaptation works**:

- Start creating in the `adaptation-works/` directory
- Use adaptation templates (nature field is `a`)
- Receive community feedback and gradually improve works
- Apply for promotion to official works after completion

This is the smoothest path to integrate into the community and understand the world. For detailed
guidance, see: [Universal Story Template Guide](docs/universal-story-template-guide-english.md)

## 7. 开始探索 / Start Exploring

### 7.1 👁️ 给读者 / For Readers

1. **选择世界观**：浏览 `worlds/` 目录下的三个世界观
2. **浏览原作**：在 `original-archives/`
   中阅读官方作品（参见[快速访问原作](#12-快速链接--quick-links)）
3. **发现改编**：探索 `adaptation-works/` 中的社区创作
4. **查看设定**：了解每个世界观的背景设定（位于 `settings/` 目录）
5. **参与反馈**：为喜欢的作品提供建设性意见

---

### 7.1 👁️ For Readers

1. **Choose a world**: Browse the three worlds in the `worlds/` directory
2. **Browse original works**: Read official works in `original-archives/` (
   see [Quick Access to Original Works](#12-快速链接--quick-links))
3. **Discover adaptations**: Explore community creations in `adaptation-works/`
4. **Check settings**: Understand the background settings of each world (located in `settings/`
   directory)
5. **Provide feedback**: Offer constructive comments for works you like

---

### 7.2 ✍️ 给创作者 / For Creators

1. **阅读指南**：查看 [贡献指南](CONTRIBUTING.md) 和 [行为准则](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
2. **学习规范**：了解 [作品命名指南](docs/work-naming-guide-chinese.md)（版本2.1.1）
3. **选择路径**：**推荐从改编作品开始**
   ，使用对应模板（参见[通用故事模板指南](docs/universal-story-template-guide-chinese.md)）
4. **开始创作**：遵循快速开始指南，创建你的故事
5. **申请晋升**：作品完成后可申请晋升为官方作品

---

### 7.2 ✍️ For Creators

1. **Read guides**: Check [Contribution Guide](CONTRIBUTING.md)
   and [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
2. **Learn standards**: Understand [Work Naming Guide](docs/work-naming-guide-english.md) (v2.1.1)
3. **Choose path**: **Recommended to start with adaptation works**, use corresponding templates (
   see [Universal Story Template Guide](docs/universal-story-template-guide-english.md))
4. **Start creating**: Follow quick start guide to create your story
5. **Apply for promotion**: Apply for promotion to official works after completion

## 8. 项目结构 / Project Structure

```txt
beastkin-shield-universe/
├── worlds/                          # 所有世界观
│   ├── beastshield/                 # 兽盾世界观
│   │   ├── original-archives/       # 原作存档（官方作品）
│   │   ├── adaptation-works/        # 改编作品（社区创作，推荐起点）
│   │   └── settings/                # 设定库
│   ├── beastshield-reforged/        # 兽盾新纪元世界观
│   └── united-beasts-alliance/      # 万兽盟世界观
├── docs/                            # 项目文档（指南、规范）
├── templates/                       # 创作模板
├── scripts/                         # 实用脚本
└── structure/                       # 结构记录（自动生成）
 ```

每个世界观都包含：Each world contains:

- `original-archives/` - 原作存档 / Original works archive
- `adaptation-works/` - 改编作品（推荐起点）/ Adaptation works (recommended start)
- `settings/` - 设定库 / Settings library

完整结构请参考：[项目结构指南](docs/project-structure-guide.md)
