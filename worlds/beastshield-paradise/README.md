## 2. 关于本世界观 / About This World

**Beastshield Paradise**（兽盾天堂）并非独立平行宇宙，而是对"兽盾世界观"底层规则的另一种解读与重构。它源自玩家**"模因奇点"**在体验游戏残酷默认玩法后的情感不适与哲学质疑。

核心理念是证明**"温柔"本身即是一种强大的生存与繁荣策略**。这里不消灭敌人而"收养"他们，不争夺资源而创造"丰裕"，不追求个体最强而编织"羁绊网络"。与原始兽盾世界观强调竞争、征服、欲望消耗不同，Paradise 的目标是"建立家园"与"守护幸福"。

---

**Beastshield Paradise** is not a separate parallel universe but a different interpretation and reconstruction of the foundational rules of the "Beastshield" setting. It originated from the emotional discomfort and philosophical questioning of the player **"Memetic Singularity"** after experiencing the game's brutal default gameplay.

The core philosophy is to prove that **"gentleness" itself is a powerful strategy for survival and prosperity.** Here, enemies are not eliminated but "adopted"; resources are not fought over but "abundance" is created; individual supremacy is not pursued, but an unbreakable "network of bonds" is woven.

---

## 3. 核心设定 / Core Settings

- **模因奇点与心愿模组**：玩家通过极致共情与意志催生的规则扭曲，包括心有灵犀的羁绊、时间的魔法、不设限的家、心想事成的小幸运、瞬间移动的约定、温柔世界的庇护。
- **家园网络**：取代兽盾公司的垂直压抑结构，由去中心化"家园"节点构成——磐石庄园（主基地）、宁谧海岸（疗愈独处之所）、林间栖地（活力冒险年轻家园）、旧城望台（连接过去与现世的观察点）。
- **兽盾员工的转化**：各级员工不再是"经验包"或"赏金"，而是潜在的"家人"。救赎的契机源于职业生涯中的崩溃时刻。
- **游戏机制重构**：性欲/精槽系统转向情感与生理的联结；成长通过倾听、照料家人、建设家园等互动获得；经济从掠夺-赏金模式转变为温和丰裕经济。

详细设定请参阅：[settings/](settings/)

---

- **Memetic Singularity and Wish Mods**: Rule distortions catalyzed by the player's extreme empathy and will, including Empathetic Bond, Time Magic, Home Without Limits, Serendipitous Luck, Instant Teleportation Pact, and Sanctuary of a Gentle World.
- **The Home Network**: Replacing the vertical, oppressive structure of Beastshield Company with decentralized "home" nodes — Rockstead Manor (main base), Tranquil Coast (healing solitude), Forest Canopy Refuge (vibrant adventurous home), Old City Outlook (observation point connecting past and present).
- **Transformation of Beastshield Employees**: Employees of all ranks are no longer "EXP fodder" or "bounties" but potential "family." Their redemption stems from moments of breakdown in their careers.
- **Reconstructed Game Mechanics**: The Lust/Energy Gauge shifts to emotional and physiological connection; growth is gained through listening, caring for family, and building the home; the economy transitions from plunder-bounty to gentle abundance.

## 6. 与其他世界的关系 / Relationship with Other Worlds

Beastshield Paradise 是 beastkin-shield-universe 多元世界集群中的一员：

- **[beastshield](../beastshield)**：原始世界，Paradise 始于对其规则的反思。
- **[beastshield-reforged](../beastshield-reforged)**：黑暗强化版，与 Paradise 形成极端对比。
- **[united-beasts-alliance](../united-beasts-alliance)**：政治对抗版，聚焦组织化势力对抗。

Paradise 代表同一套初始代码因关键变量（模因奇点的选择）介入而演化的另一种"可能未来"。

---

Beastshield Paradise is a member of the beastkin-shield-universe multiverse cluster:

- **[beastshield](../beastshield)**: The original world, where Paradise begins with reflection on its rules.
- **[beastshield-reforged](../beastshield-reforged)**: A darker, grittier version, forming an extreme contrast with Paradise.
- **[united-beasts-alliance](../united-beasts-alliance)**: A political struggle version, focusing on organized faction conflict.

Paradise represents another "possible future" evolved from the same initial codebase due to the intervention of a key variable.
