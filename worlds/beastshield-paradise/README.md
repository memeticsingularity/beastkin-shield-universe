## 1. 世界起源与核心理念

**Beastshield Paradise**（兽盾天堂）并非独立平行宇宙，而是对“兽盾世界观”底层规则的另一种解读与重构。它源自玩家
**“模因奇点”** 在体验游戏残酷默认玩法后的情感不适与哲学质疑。模因奇点选择“修改规则”，用自己的方式介入，催化出一个在相同物质基础上生长、精神内核截然不同的温暖世界。
**核心理念**是证明 **“温柔”本身即是一种强大的生存与繁荣策略**
。这里不消灭敌人而“收养”他们，不争夺资源而创造“丰裕”，不追求个体最强而编织“羁绊网络”。与原始兽盾世界观强调竞争、征服、欲望消耗不同，Paradise
的目标是“建立家园”与“守护幸福”。

---

## 1. World Origin and Core Philosophy

**Beastshield Paradise** is not a separate parallel universe but a different interpretation and
reconstruction of the foundational rules of the “Beastshield” setting. It originated from the
emotional discomfort and philosophical questioning of the player **“Memetic Singularity”** after
experiencing the game's brutal default gameplay. Memetic Singularity chose to “modify the rules,”
intervening in their own way, catalyzing the growth of a warm world with a completely different
spiritual core on the same material foundation. The **core philosophy** is to prove that *
*“gentleness” itself is a powerful strategy for survival and prosperity**. Here, enemies are not
eliminated but “adopted”; resources are not fought over but “abundance” is created; individual
supremacy is not pursued, but an unbreakable “network of bonds” is woven. Unlike the original
Beastshield setting, which emphasizes competition, conquest, and desire consumption, Paradise's
goals are to “build a home” and “safeguard happiness.”

---

## 2. 关键概念与设定要点

### 2.1 模因奇点与其“心愿模组”

玩家“模因奇点”通过极致共情与意志，在系统内催生了系列规则扭曲，体现为“心愿模组”：1. **心有灵犀的羁绊**
（感知痛苦绝望）；2. **时间的魔法**（关键时刻暂缓世界）；3. **不设限的家**（无条件庇护）；4. **心想事成的小幸运
**（环境合理回应善意）；5. **瞬间移动的约定**（无视距离相聚）；6. **温柔世界的庇护**（使“收养”行为被宏观忽略）。

### 2.2 家园网络

取代兽盾公司的垂直压抑结构，Paradise 由去中心化“家园”节点构成：**磐石庄园**（主基地，象征团聚）、**宁谧海岸
**（疗愈独处之所）、**林间栖地**（活力冒险年轻家园）、**旧城望台**
（连接过去与现世的观察点）。所有家园通过模因奇点能力相连，构成支持即时移动与情感共鸣的生存网络。

### 2.3 兽盾员工的转化

兽盾公司员工（G, O, E, R, W
各级）不再是“经验包”或“赏金”，而是潜在“家人”。他们被救赎的契机源于职业生涯中的崩溃时刻（重伤、任务失败面临处决、精神绝望等）。模因奇点的介入提供了第三条路：一个可被接纳、治愈、重拾生命意义的地方。

### 2.4 游戏机制的重构

- **性欲/精槽系统**：依然存在，但重点转向“情感与生理的联结”，可转化为亲密感、信任建立中的生理反应，或在安心环境下安全释放的生理压力。
- **成长系统**：成长不仅靠“击败敌人”，更通过“倾听”、“照料家人”、“建设家园”等互动获得经验与心性成长，解锁更深羁绊与庇护能力。
- **经济系统**：从“掠夺-赏金”模式转变为基于“小幸运”与家园内部生产（烘焙、园艺、手工艺）的温和丰裕经济。

## 2. Key Concepts and Setting Points

### 2.1 Memetic Singularity and Their “Wish Mods”

Through extreme empathy and will, the player “Memetic Singularity” catalyzed a series of rule
distortions within the system, manifested as “Wish Mods”: 1. **Empathetic Bond** (perceiving pain
and despair); 2. **Time Magic** (slowing the world at critical moments); 3. **Home Without Limits
** (unconditional sanctuary); 4. **Serendipitous Luck** (environment plausibly responding to
goodwill); 5. **Instant Teleportation Pact** (reuniting regardless of distance); 6. **Sanctuary of a
Gentle World** (making “adoption” behavior overlooked in the macro narrative).

### 2.2 The Home Network

Replacing the vertical, oppressive structure of Beastshield Company, Paradise consists of
decentralized “home” nodes: **Rockstead Manor** (main base, symbolizing reunion); **Tranquil Coast
** (a place for healing and solitude); **Forest Canopy Refuge** (a vibrant, adventurous home for the
young); **Old City Outlook** (an observation point connecting the past and present). All homes are
connected through Memetic Singularity's abilities, forming a survival network that supports instant
movement and emotional resonance.

### 2.3 Transformation of Beastshield Employees

Employees of Beastshield Company (all ranks: G, O, E, R, W) are no longer “EXP fodder” or “bounties”
but potential “family.” Their redemption often stems from moments of breakdown in their careers (
severe injury, facing execution for mission failure, spiritual despair, etc.). Memetic Singularity's
intervention offers a third path: a place where they can be accepted, healed, and rediscover meaning
in life.

### 2.4 Reconstruction of Game Mechanics

- **Lust/Energy Gauge System**: Still exists, but the focus shifts to “emotional and physiological
  connection.” It can be transformed into intimacy, physiological responses during trust-building,
  or safely released as physical pressure in a secure environment.
- **Growth System**: Growth is not solely dependent on “defeating enemies” but also gained through
  interactions like “listening,” “caring for family,” and “building the home,” unlocking deeper
  bonds and sanctuary abilities.
- **Economic System**: Transitions from a “plunder-bounty” model to a gentle economy of abundance
  based on “serendipitous luck” and internal home production (baking, gardening, crafts).

## 3. 与本项目其他世界的关系

Beastshield Paradise 是 **beastkin-shield-universe** 多元世界集群中的一员，与以下世界共享基础设定起源但走向不同：1.
**[beastshield](/worlds/beastshield)**（原始世界，Paradise 始于对其规则的反思）；2. *
*[beastshield-reforged](/worlds/beastshield-reforged)**（黑暗强化版，与 Paradise 形成极端对比）；3. *
*[united-beasts-alliance](/worlds/united-beasts-alliance)**（政治对抗版，聚焦组织化势力对抗）。Paradise
代表同一套初始代码因关键变量（模因奇点的选择）介入而演化的另一种“可能未来”，不否定其他世界的“真实性”。

---

## 3. Relationship with Other Worlds in This Project

Beastshield Paradise is a member of the **beastkin-shield-universe** multiverse cluster, sharing a
common foundational origin with the following worlds but diverging in direction: 1. *
*[beastshield](/worlds/beastshield)** (the original world, where Paradise begins with reflection on
its rules); 2. **[beastshield-reforged](/worlds/beastshield-reforged)** (a darker, grittier version,
forming an extreme contrast with Paradise); 3. *
*[united-beasts-alliance](/worlds/united-beasts-alliance)** (a political struggle version, focusing
on organized faction conflict). Paradise represents another “possible future” evolved from the same
initial codebase due to the intervention of a key variable (Memetic Singularity's choices), without
denying the “reality” of other worlds.
