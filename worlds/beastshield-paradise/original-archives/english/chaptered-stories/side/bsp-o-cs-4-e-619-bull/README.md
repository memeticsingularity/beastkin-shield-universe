## Synopsis / 故事简介

In the abandoned slaughterhouse on the eastern outskirts of White Capital, the air still carries the mixed scent of rust and stale blood. Bounty hunter Zhao Yan is tracking a bounty target when he encounters a black-uniformed bovine combat soldier—Tieti. Unlike Leishan, this bovine beastkin isn't seeking help; instead, he is repeatedly smashing his fists into an abandoned hydraulic press, as if punishing himself. Among his chips lies a past of blood debt and atonement.

白之都东郊的废弃屠宰场，空气中残留着铁锈与陈旧血液的混合气味。赏金猎人赵岩正在追踪一个悬赏目标，却遇见了一个黑制服的牛兽人武斗兵——铁蹄。与雷山不同，这位牛兽人并非主动求援，而是在用自己的拳头一遍遍砸向废弃的液压机，仿佛在惩罚自己。他的筹码里，藏着一段血债与赎罪的往事。
