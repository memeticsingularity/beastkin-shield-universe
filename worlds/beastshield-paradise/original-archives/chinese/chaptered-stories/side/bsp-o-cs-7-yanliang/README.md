## 故事简介 / Synopsis

炎与魉是一对赏金猎人搭档，专门狩猎兽盾公司的守卫以换取赏金。炎擅长以灼流暗劲快速制服目标，性格冷酷高效；魉拥有变色龙先天隐身天赋，负责善后与记录编号，性格温和善良。两人性格迥异却配合默契，在兽盾新兵训练基地展开了一场惊心动魄的猎杀行动。

Yan and Liang are a bounty hunter duo specializing in hunting Beastshield Company guards for rewards. Yan excels at quickly subduing targets with Scorching Flow Dark Force, possessing a cold and efficient personality; Liang has a chameleon's innate invisibility talent, responsible for cleanup and recording sock numbers, with a gentle and kind nature. The two have contrasting personalities yet work in perfect harmony, launching a thrilling hunting operation at the Beastshield recruit training base.
