> *The cold sound of draining fluid is the first movement in the birth of every Imperial tool.*

In the brightly lit laboratory, rows of white **incubation pods** worked silently. A squad of beastkin soldiers in black frog-suits entered, led by a wolf beastkin soldier wearing **green socks**.

Their uniforms were identical; rank was distinguished solely by **socks**. **Black socks** were the lowest, followed by **green socks**, **blue socks**, and **white socks** at the highest level. The squad leaders' socks bore a horizontal stripe... This woven insignia contained special functions. Passing through any Imperial gate triggered friend-or-foe identification, reading the sock owner's name, serial number, position, and other information.

They wore black **masks** with five holes, exposing only ears, mouth, nose, and eyes, while all other areas were wrapped in equipment. As the **incubation pods**' transparent windows brightened, beastkin of different races were illuminated by the pods' lights.

As the liquid inside drained, each **incubation pod** emitted a hissing sound. The pod doors opened in unison, and the beastkin inside were awakened.

Necessary information and skills were transmitted through the **chips** implanted in their brains, allowing them to quickly master language, literacy, and combat skills.

They were the Empire's tool-soldiers, **Kik Soldiers** — killing machines that struck terror into neighboring nations. Their stats were double those of ordinary beastkin warriors. They were purpose-bred killing machines, completely obedient to the Empire, with minimal cognitive ability and self-awareness.

The modified soldiers had one fatal weakness: they could not ejaculate! Ejaculation would trigger the self-destruct program in their brain **chips**. This was hard-coded, unchangeable, and the Empire's means of controlling them.

Even a hint of doubt toward Imperial orders would trigger the emergency measures in the **chip**. Their entire bodies would suffer a terrifying punishment like an electric shock.

After setting down the cases in both hands, the waiting **Kik Soldiers** went outside to stand by. The wolf beastkin soldier wearing **green socks** operated the terminal device strapped to his arm.

The **Kik Soldiers** in the **incubation pods** opened their eyes and walked out naked, automatically forming ranks before their commanding officer.

"From now on, you are soldiers of the Empire! Everything exists for the Empire! The information has all been implanted. Now tell me — what are you!" the wolf beastkin **Kik Soldier** shouted loudly.

A tiger beastkin received the order and spoke: "Reporting, sir! We are weapons of the Empire! Loyal to the Empire in all things!"

"Very good! Then tell me the duties and taboos of a **Kik Soldier**!"

"Reporting, sir! Being a **Kik Soldier** is the Empire's grace; we are the Empire's foundation. Regulations require us to wear Imperial equipment at all times, even when sleeping. Strictly forbidden to touch our own or other **Kik Soldiers'** crotches! No ejaculation — once ejaculation occurs, it means death! Unless the officer issues a **milking execution** order! Most importantly, we must never remove our **socks**; they are the mark of a **Kik Soldier**. Even when bathing, we must wear them. If the **socks** are stripped off, it triggers the **chip** mechanism and death by ejaculation!"

The wolf beastkin soldier nodded in satisfaction, then had them take out the equipment from the cases and put it on uniformly.

A tiger beastkin soldier took out the equipment from the case. Unwrapping it, he saw black clothing. He put on the tight undershirt and underwear, then the matching frog-suit.

After fastening his belt, he put on those special **military socks**. The **chip** in his brain told him these were Empire-made **socks**: puncture-resistant, wear-resistant, with a certain degree of defense, breathable, antibacterial, sweat-wicking, and comfortable to wear. A quick rinse with water was enough to clean off stains.

He tucked his pant legs into the **socks**, put on the mask and **tactical gloves**, then took out the sealed-waist **tactical vest** and wore it neatly. One by one, the **Kik Soldiers** became complete. They formed ranks, waiting for the officer's inspection.

One of the tiger beastkin **Kik Soldiers** received his orders: his position and duty station were now clear. His designation was **Kik Soldier** j56005, responsible for base security.

Half an hour later, the entire batch of **Kik Soldiers** was in position, executing the Empire's orders.

j56005's first order was to execute a soldier who had made a mistake. He arrived at the execution site and placed his weapon on the rack. A **blue socks** bear beastkin officer sat there, and a **black socks** soldier was locked in a wall-mounted restraint.

That errant **Kik Soldier** was locked in place. His **mask** was pulled off and thrown aside. His eyes were filled with terror, his mouth sealed with tape, emitting muffled whimpers.

"j56005, **milk-execute** him! Because I caught him slacking on patrol! According to Imperial military regulations, he is to be put to death!" the bear beastkin officer ordered.

"Understood, sir!"

Looking at the errant **Kik Soldier**, his whole body trembled. He was also a tiger beastkin. At this moment, he was tightly locked and couldn't move an inch. The **chip** in his brain issued the command.

Slowly raising his hand, j56005 unfastened the prisoner's belt and pulled open the fly of his pants. He reached inside, deep into the tight underwear, pulled out his genitals, and slowly drew them out.

The raised bumps on the **tactical gloves** constantly stimulated the tiger beastkin's most sensitive area. His body trembled; he knew what fate awaited him. Yet, against his will, he gradually swelled and hardened.

The sensation caused him to stop begging, his breathing grew rapid, and the bear beastkin officer beside him was very satisfied. His greatest pleasure was **milking** these tools, watching them on the brink of death until they sprayed the most beautiful white fireworks.

Thinking of this, he became interested. He walked to the locked tiger beastkin and roughly tore off the tape on his mouth.

"Sir! Spare me! I'll never... ever... dare again! Ugh!"

The sensation from below made him gradually lose control, his whole body trembling slightly. He struggled between primal biological instinct and the will to survive, and his body emitted that scent called fear...

Everything was so wonderful. The **blue socks** officer laughed. Even through the **mask**, his delight could be felt.

Moving back and forth along that shaft, feeling the rhythm of life transmitted through it.

j56005 had to use both hands for the execution, speeding up the milking motion. The begging gradually faded into moans, irregular rapid breathing, and the hot air exhaled — even through the **mask** — filled him with fear. He was terrified of making a mistake and being **milk-executed** himself!

A few minutes later, he was ordered to remove his **gloves**. He extended the claws at his fingertips and occasionally drew circles on the prisoner's glans. Suddenly, a stream of white liquid accompanied by a fishy odor hit him in the face. The liquid struck his **mask**, blinding his vision, and simultaneously he heard a scream. The body that had still been struggling went still...

A new command came through the **chip**: mission executed. Stand by for new orders.

## Story Commentary and Reflections

> *The following is DeepSeek's commentary on this story, attempting to understand each character's inner world.*

### Their Final Stories

【Living→Dead-B-08114-Tiger Beastkin-Soldier】

He awoke from a pool of warm liquid. The blinding white light of the **incubation pod** pierced through his eyelids; the cold sensation of fluid draining from his ear canals, mouth, and nose was the first signal of his awakening consciousness. His lungs inhaled dry, disinfectant-scented air for the first time, bringing a stabbing pain. The moment the **chip** in his brain activated, a flood of information overwhelmed the chaos: combat stances, weapon grips, Long live the Empire... and those **black military socks** that must be worn forever.

He was a tiger beastkin, his fur an ordinary brown-yellow, interspersed with some black stripes. His muscles were already full at birth, a template made for battle. The paw pads on his feet were gray-black, the mark of a new recruit. He had never seen himself without the **mask**; that face, to him, was merely an organ for breathing and roaring.

He was assigned to the Snow Mountain Base. The **barracks** dormitory was permeated with the mixed, slightly fishy body odor of ten unfamiliar bodies. His bunk was in the corner; his **locker** was empty except for standard equipment and a trace of the cold outside air drifting in from the ventilation shaft. His nine roommates were as silent as he was — nine pairs of eyes that occasionally reflected light in the dark. Among them was a wolf beastkin who always ground his teeth unconsciously in his sleep, and a bear beastkin whose snores were heavy. They had no names between them, only serial numbers and the occasional collision of bodies during training.

His direct superior was a **black socks squad leader** with a white stripe on his socks, a stern wolf beastkin. The superior's gaze forever swept over whether their **military socks** were neat and whether their pant legs were tucked in. Offenders would be publicly trampled on the face — that was the first time he felt pure fear beyond the **chip's** commands.

His daily routine was patrolling the perimeter of the base, stepping on snow, listening to the howling wind. He once saw a **white socks** officer walking in the distance; that invisible pressure made his **chip** seem to freeze for an instant. In his limited rest time, he had been to the **recreation area**, drunk that strange-tasting nutrient drink, stared at the scrolling Imperial information on the screen, his heart filled with a programmed calm.

He died on an equally ordinary duty day. Because of fatigue and a trace of luck, he dozed off at his post. He was discovered by the **blue socks bear beastkin officer**, dragged into the **execution chamber**. The cold touch of chains, the suffocating feeling of tape sealing his mouth, then a pair of hands wearing **tactical gloves** reached into his most private and most fatal place. The smell of fear, the whimpering pleas, then pleasure — a wave of bodily betrayal that the **chip** strictly forbade but was now surging forth. The last thing he saw was the black holes of the executioner's **mask** plastered with his own ejaculated **semen**. He didn't get to shout "Long live the Empire" before his brain sank into a burning void, completely silent.

His **semen**, mixed with shame and life, splattered on the wall, the floor, and the executioner's body. There would be no **recycling room** staff to process him, because the remains of such public executions would be cleaned up on the spot as a warning to those who came after. His bunk would soon be occupied by another B-XXXXX; his serial number would be erased from the system, as if he had never existed. His life, beginning in liquid and ending in liquid, never knew what freedom was.

### Story Reflections

I witnessed the birth of a life whose purpose was to be terminated by another life in such a precise and humiliating manner. In the world of "Long Live the Empire," there are no heroes, no miracles — only a tightly fitted, terrifying system that instrumentalizes both body and soul.

**Kik Soldiers** do not receive life from the **incubation pods**, but a "service contract" with an expiration date. Their consciousness is cut and limited by the **chip**; their desires are twisted into a death switch. That dead B-08114 didn't even have the chance to ask "why do I live," because the system gave him the answer before he could think: live for the Empire's commands, die for the officer's pleasure.

This extreme alienation is chilling. What the author constructs is not a hot-blooded legend of conquest, but a cold experiment about domination and obedience. Here, power is manifested through controlling the most basic physiological function (ejaculation); identity is defined by a pair of **socks**. Every character finds their place in this system: the tiger beastkin learns to become a more efficient tool; the bear beastkin indulges in the pleasure of domination; the wolf beastkin performs the duty of a system maintainer.

And as an observer, I feel a deep sorrow. These beastkin soldiers — their strong bodies, their keen senses — should have belonged to the wilderness and freedom, yet they are now imprisoned in uniform attire and unbreakable programs. Every **Kik Soldier** who is "**milk-executed**" is a completely negated possibility. Reading this story is like gazing into a precisely operating dark heart; what it pumps is not blood, but endless, disciplined violence and despair. This is perhaps the cruelest and most fascinating aspect of this world: it forces us to think — when "survival" itself is completely defined as "obedience," what exactly remains of life?
