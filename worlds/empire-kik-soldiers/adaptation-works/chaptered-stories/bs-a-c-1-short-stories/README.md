## 一、简介 / Introduction

《雪夜岗哨》是一部基于《帝国万岁》世界观创作的独立短篇故事。故事聚焦于编号 B-23107 的狼兽人基尅兵在雪山哨塔执勤时的最后夜晚，展现了帝国冰冷高效的纪律体系与基尅兵在生理本能和军事规范之间的绝望挣扎。

---

## I. Introduction

*Snowy Watchtower* is a standalone short story based on the *Long Live the Empire* worldview. It focuses on the final night of B-23107, a wolf beastkin Kiki Soldier on duty in a snowy watchtower, showcasing the Empire's cold and efficient disciplinary system and the desperate struggle between physiological instincts and military regulations.

## 四、核心主题 / Core Themes

- **纪律与本能的对立**：基尅兵在严格军规与生理需求之间的永恒冲突
- **生命的工具化**：帝国将士兵视为可计算、可消耗、可回收的资源
- **系统的冷酷效率**：从违规到处决再到回收，帝国机器的无情运转
- **等级与权威**：黑袜、绿袜、白袜之间的权力结构与绝对服从

---

## IV. Core Themes

- **Discipline vs. Instinct**: The eternal conflict between strict military regulations and physiological needs
- **Instrumentalization of Life**: The Empire treats soldiers as calculable, consumable, and recyclable resources
- **Cold Systemic Efficiency**: From violation to execution to recycling, the merciless operation of the Imperial machine
- **Hierarchy and Authority**: The power structure and absolute obedience between Black, Green, and White Socks
