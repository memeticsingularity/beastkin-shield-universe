# 中文

## **Part I: 关于本项目**

本项目旨在对 **笑天神犬 / 红鼻子黄犬** 老师原创的奇幻小说《**万兽盟**》进行系统化的数字整理与呈现。

目前，项目已收录并完成了 **第一卷** 前三章的标准化英文格式转换、章节导航与评述撰写。所有工作均严格遵守原作的设定与精神，力求为读者提供清晰、连贯且富有深度的阅读体验。

**已完成内容概览：**

* **第一卷 (Volume 1):** 讲述了主角 **辛** 从温馨日常堕入黑暗，并踏上复仇之路的起始篇章。
    * **第01章 日常与离别**: 介绍**辛**与叔叔**奥克**的亲情。
    * **第02章 天真吞没**: 描绘**辛**收到噩耗后血腥的蜕变仪式。
    * **第03章 血试炼**: **辛**三年后挑战**护盟天卫斩峰**，通过残酷试炼加入**兽盟堡**。

**核心整理原则：**

1. **忠于原著**：所有情节、角色与设定均源自原作，未做任何修改。
2. **格式统一**：采用标准化的Markdown模板，确保章节结构、角色名与专有名词（如**兽盟堡**、**狼风谷**）标注清晰。
3. **深度评述**：每章附有独立的“故事评述与感慨”，以白话文和英文解析角色命运与故事内涵。
4. **阅读友好**：提供完整的章节导航（上一章/目录/下一章）与分级提示。

## **Part II: 内容分级与阅读指引**

**⚠️ 内容分级警告 ⚠️**

本故事包含以下成人内容，建议读者根据自身情况审慎阅读：

* **暴力与创伤描写**：包含战斗、死亡、断肢、血腥场面及可能引起不适的行为描写（如生食）。
* **心理冲击**：着重描绘角色经历重大创伤（如至亲惨死）后的心理崩溃、极端化转变与复仇执念。
* **世界观特有主题**：探讨由**雄兽人**（兼具双重性征）构成的独特社会中，关于亲情、成长与力量的复杂关系。

**各章节分级细化：**

* **第01章**: R15 (温和场景，情感铺垫)

* **第03章**: R18 (战斗伤害，关节损伤，撕咬见血)

**推荐读者年龄：18岁以上**

所有内容均为虚构创作，旨在塑造人物与推动剧情。阅读时请保持理性，区分虚构与现实。

# English Version

## Part I: About This Project

This project aims to systematically digitize and present the fantasy novel “**United Beasts Alliance
**” (**万兽盟**), originally created by **Laughing Sky Divine Dog / Red Nose Yellow Dog**.

Currently, the project has compiled, standardized, and presented the first three chapters of *
*Volume 1** in English, complete with formatted text, chapter navigation, and reflective commentary.
All work strictly adheres to the original story's setting and spirit, striving to provide readers
with a clear, coherent, and insightful reading experience.

**Overview of Completed Content:**

* **Volume 1:** Chronicles the protagonist **Xin**'s fall from a peaceful life into darkness and the
  beginning of his path to vengeance.
    * **Chapter 01: Everyday Life and Farewell:** Introduces **Xin** and his uncle **Oak**.
    * **Chapter 02: The Devouring of Innocence:** Depicts **Xin**'s bloody transformation after
      receiving tragic news.
    * **Chapter 03: Trial by Blood:** **Xin** challenges the elite guard **Zhan Feng** three years
      later, passing a brutal trial to join the **Beast Alliance Fortress**.

**Core Principles:**

1. **Fidelity to the Source:** All plots, characters, and settings are derived from the original
   work without alteration.
2. **Format Standardization:** Utilizes standardized Markdown templates ensuring clear chapter
   structure, with character names and proper nouns (e.g., **Beast Alliance Fortress**, **Wolf Wind
   Valley**) consistently bolded.
3. **In-Depth Commentary:** Each chapter includes an independent “Story Commentary and Reflection”
   section, analyzing character destinies and narrative themes in both plain Chinese and English.
4. **Reader-Friendly:** Provides full chapter navigation (Previous/Index/Next) and content ratings.

## Part II: Content Rating & Reading Guide

**⚠️ CONTENT RATING WARNING ⚠️**

This story contains adult content. Reader discretion is advised.

* **Violence & Trauma:** Contains depictions of combat, death, dismemberment, gore, and potentially
  disturbing acts (e.g., consumption of raw flesh).
* **Psychological Impact:** Focuses on psychological breakdown, extreme transformation, and
  obsessive vengeance following severe trauma (e.g., brutal death of a loved one).
* **World-Specific Themes:** Explores complex relationships involving family, growth, and power
  within a unique society of **male beastmen** (possessing dual characteristics).

**Chapter-Specific Ratings:**

* **Chapter 01:** R15 (Mild scenes, emotional setup)
* **Chapter 02:** R18 (Psychological shock, bloody ritual, self-harm)
* **Chapter 03:** R18 (Combat injuries, joint dislocation, biting that draws blood)

**Recommended Reader Age: 18+**

All content is fictional, created for character development and plot progression. Please maintain a
rational perspective while reading and distinguish between fiction and reality.
