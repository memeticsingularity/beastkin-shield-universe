## 2. 关于本世界观 / About This World

《万兽盟》是由**笑天神犬 / 红鼻子黄犬**创作的奇幻小说。项目目前收录并完成了第一卷前三章的标准化整理，讲述了主角**辛**从温馨日常堕入黑暗，并踏上复仇之路的起始篇章。

这是一个独立且完整的奇幻世界观，与作者其他作品（如《兽盾公司》系列）在剧情、角色、设定与世界观上均无任何关联。请勿将不同作品的世界观进行混淆。

---

*United Beasts Alliance* is a fantasy novel created by **Laughing Sky Divine Dog / Red Nose Yellow Dog.** The project has currently compiled and standardized the first three chapters of Volume 1, chronicling the protagonist **Xin's** fall from a peaceful life into darkness and the beginning of his path to vengeance.

This is an independent and complete fantasy setting with no narrative, character, setting, or worldview connections to the author's other works (such as the "Beast Shield Corporation" series).

---

## 3. 核心设定 / Core Settings

- **兽盟堡**：万兽盟的核心要塞，辛通过血试炼后加入的组织据点。
- **雄兽人社会**：由兼具双重性征的雄兽人构成的独特社会，探讨亲情、成长与力量的复杂关系。
- **护盟天卫**：兽盟堡的精英守卫，辛的血试炼对手斩峰即属此列。
- **狼风谷**：故事中的重要地点，与辛的身世和命运紧密相连。

详细设定请参阅：[settings/](settings/)

---

- **Beast Alliance Fortress**: The core stronghold of the United Beasts Alliance, the organization Xin joins after passing the blood trial.
- **Male Beastman Society**: A unique society of male beastmen possessing dual characteristics, exploring complex relationships involving family, growth, and power.
- **Elite Fortress Guard**: The elite defenders of the Beast Alliance Fortress; Zhan Feng, Xin's blood trial opponent, belongs to this order.
- **Wolf Wind Valley**: An important location in the story, closely tied to Xin's origins and destiny.

For detailed settings, see: [settings/](settings/)
