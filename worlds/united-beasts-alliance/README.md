# 中文

## **Part I: 关于原作**

《万兽盟》是一部架构宏大、情感细腻的兽人题材奇幻小说，讲述了一个在独特兽人文明社会中，关于**亲情、仇恨、成长与复仇
**的史诗故事。

在这个世界里，兽人建立了以“兽盟城”为中心的城邦社会，拥有“兽盟堡”、“卫兵制度”、“医疗体系”等成熟的社会结构。故事的核心围绕着主角
**辛**——一名因体质瘦弱而梦想成为医疗师的年轻虎兽人展开。在抚养他长大的叔叔**奥克**
惨遭杀害后，辛的世界彻底崩塌。为了追查真凶、为至亲复仇，他被迫唤醒血脉中的野性，以决绝的姿态踏入兽盟堡的卫兵选拔，并由此卷入一场深不可测的阴谋与力量漩涡。

小说的魅力在于其对兽人社会细节的生动刻画、对角色内心转变的深刻描摹，以及充满张力与策略的战斗场面。

## **Part II: 分级与内容提示**

**内容分级警告**

本作品包含以下内容，建议读者根据自身情况斟酌阅读：

* **暴力与创伤性场面**：包含战斗、死亡、断肢及其他可能引起不适的暴力描写。
* **心理冲击与角色黑化**：描绘了角色经历重大创伤（如亲人惨死）后的心理崩溃与行为极端化（如生食等）。
* **轻度成人主题**：涉及兽人社会独特的性别与家庭观念探讨。

**推荐读者年龄：16岁以上**

所有内容均为虚构创作，旨在推进剧情与塑造人物。请您在阅读时保持理性，将虚构与现实区分开来。

# English Version

## Part I: About the Original Work

"Ten Thousand Beasts Alliance" is a meticulously crafted, emotionally rich fantasy novel set in a
unique beastkin civilization. It tells an epic tale of **family, hatred, growth, and revenge**
within a structured beastman society.

In this world, beastkin have established a city-state society centered around the "Beast Alliance
City," featuring mature social structures such as the "Beast Alliance Fortress," a "Guard System,"
and a "Medical System." The story revolves around the protagonist, **Xin** — a young tiger beastkin
of frail physique who dreams of becoming a medical practitioner. His world shatters when his beloved
uncle and guardian, **Aoke**, is brutally murdered. To track down the culprit and avenge his kin,
Xin is forced to awaken the primal ferocity within his bloodline. With unwavering determination, he
steps into the Beast Alliance Fortress's guard selection, plunging himself into an abyss of
unfathomable conspiracy and clashes of power.

The novel's appeal lies in its vivid depiction of beastkin societal details, profound portrayal of
characters' internal transformations, and tense, strategically charged combat scenes.

## Part II: Content Rating and Warnings

**Content Rating Warning**

This work contains the following elements. Readers are advised to consider their own preferences:

* **Violence and Traumatic Scenes**: Includes depictions of combat, death, dismemberment, and other
  violent descriptions that may cause discomfort.
* **Psychological Impact and Character Corruption**: Portrays psychological breakdown and extreme
  behavioral shifts (e.g., consumption of raw flesh) following significant trauma (such as the
  brutal death of a loved one).
* **Mild Adult Themes**: Explores unique concepts of gender and family within beastkin society.

**Recommended Reader Age: 16+**

All content is fictional, created to advance the plot and develop characters. Please maintain a
rational perspective while reading and distinguish between fiction and reality.
