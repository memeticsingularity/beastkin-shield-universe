## 二、关于本世界观 / About This World

**温暖玩家篇章**

《奇克兽士乐园》是《帝国万岁》的温暖向对应版本。聚焦于 Kik 士兵在和平时期的日常、友谊与成长故事。与《帝国万岁》的黑暗科幻基调形成对比，本世界观更注重角色之间的温情互动与日常生活。

---

## I. About This World

**Warm Player Stories**

Paradise Kik Soldiers is the warm-hearted counterpart to Empire Kik Soldiers. It focuses on the daily lives, friendships, and growth stories of Kik soldiers during peacetime. Contrasting with the dark sci-fi tone of Empire Kik Soldiers, this world emphasizes warm character interactions and everyday life.

---

## 三、核心设定 / Core Settings

- **Kik 士兵**：经过基因改造的兽人士兵，在和平时期融入普通社会生活
- **乐园设施**：为退役或休假的 Kik 士兵提供的康复与娱乐场所
- **日常主题**：聚焦于训练之余的友情、恋爱与生活琐事

详细设定请参阅：[settings/](settings/)

## II. Core Settings

- **Kik Soldiers**: Genetically modified beastkin soldiers integrating into civilian life during peacetime
- **Paradise Facilities**: Rehabilitation and recreation centers for retired or on-leave Kik soldiers
