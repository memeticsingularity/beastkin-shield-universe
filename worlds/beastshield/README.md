## 2. 关于本世界观 / About This World

《兽盾》是一部设定独特的兽人题材叙事作品。故事发生在一个由**兽盾公司**掌控的世界中。该公司势力庞大，几乎匹敌政府，从事军火走私、违禁品贩卖等非法活动。其员工均为兽人，身穿紧身制服，行事残酷，内部等级森严。

故事以一名玩家通过VR游戏进入这个世界为开端。在这个世界里，所有攻击造成的伤害都会转化为目标的**性欲值**，当性欲值达到上限时，角色会**喷射死亡**。玩家需要利用这一独特机制进行战斗、潜行与探索，并与兽盾公司的员工们展开各种互动。

---

*BeastShield* is a unique furry-themed narrative work set in a world dominated by the **BeastShield Corporation.** The company's power rivals that of the government, engaged in illegal activities such as arms smuggling and contraband trafficking. Its employees are all beastmen, clad in tight uniforms, operating under a brutal and strictly hierarchical system.

The story begins with a player entering this world through a VR game. Here, all damage inflicted is converted into the target's **Libido Value.** When this value reaches its limit, the character ejaculates to death. Players must utilize this unique mechanic to fight, stealth, explore, and interact with the employees of the BeastShield Corporation.

## 3. 核心设定 / Core Settings

- **兽盾公司**：游戏内的主要反派组织，势力滔天，纪律严酷而混乱。员工之间缺乏怜悯，实行包食宿，设有新兵营、体训房、大食堂及地下格斗场。
- **员工等级**：按制服颜色和职责分为 G级杂兵（绿）、O级监工（蓝）、E级武斗兵（黑）、R级枪械兵（白）、W级摔跤兵（深蓝）。员工唯一身份标识是印在长筒袜袜底的编号。
- **游戏机制**：**性欲值系统**——所有伤害转化为目标的性欲值，蓄满时目标射精死亡；**成长系统**——通过处决敌人获得经验升级；**随从/换装/隐藏资料**等特殊系统。
- **主要地点**：白之都、兽盾总部大厦、地下格斗场、初始仓库、荒野与道路。

详细设定请参阅：[settings/](settings/)

---

- **The BeastShield Corporation**: The primary antagonist faction wielding immense power, with harsh yet chaotic discipline. Employees show little pity for each other; facilities include a boot camp, training gym, cafeteria, and underground fighting arena.
- **Employee Ranks**: Ranked by uniform color and duty — G-Level Grunt (green), O-Level Overseer (blue), E-Level Enforcer (black), R-Level Ranged (white), W-Level Wrestler (dark blue). The only identifier is a number printed on the sole of their long socks.
- **Game Mechanics**: **Libido System** — all damage is converted into the target's libido, upon reaching the limit the target ejaculates to death; **Progression System** — gain XP by executing enemies, level up; **Companion/Disguise/Hidden Profiles** special systems.
- **Key Locations**: The White City, BeastShield Headquarters, Underground Fighting Arena, Starting Warehouse, Wilderness & Road.

For detailed settings, see: [settings/](settings/)
