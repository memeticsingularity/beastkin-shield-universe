## 1. 关于原作 / About the Original Work

《兽盾》是一部设定独特的兽人题材叙事作品。故事发生在一个由“兽盾公司”掌控的世界中。该公司势力庞大，几乎匹敌政府，从事军火走私、违禁品贩卖等非法活动。其员工均为兽人，身穿紧身制服，行事残酷，内部等级森严。

故事以一名玩家通过VR游戏进入这个世界为开端。在这个世界里，所有攻击造成的伤害都会转化为目标的“性欲值”，当性欲值达到上限时，角色会“喷射死亡”。玩家需要利用这一独特机制进行战斗、潜行与探索，并与兽盾公司的员工们展开各种互动。

*“BeastShield” is a unique furry-themed narrative work set in a world dominated by the "BeastShield
Corporation." The company's power rivals that of the government, engaged in illegal activities such
as arms smuggling and contraband trafficking. Its employees are all beastmen, clad in tight
uniforms, operating under a brutal and strictly hierarchical system.*

*The story begins with a player entering this world through a VR game. Here, all damage inflicted is
converted into the target's "Libido Value." When this value reaches its limit, the character "
ejaculates to death." Players must utilize this unique mechanic to fight, stealth, explore, and
interact with the employees of the BeastShield Corporation.*

## 2. 核心设定 / Core Setting

### 2.1 兽盾公司 / The BeastShield Corporation

- **性质**：游戏内的主要反派组织，势力滔天，纪律严酷而混乱。
- **文化**：员工之间缺乏怜悯，嘲笑同事死亡。实行包食宿，设有新兵营、体训房、大食堂及地下格斗场。
- **管理**：干部可随意处罚甚至处决下属。过道设有巨型垃圾桶，疑似用于处理员工尸体。
- **标识**：员工唯一身份标识是印在长筒袜袜底的编号，这也是击败他们后兑换赏金（撸币）的凭证。

*Nature: The primary antagonist faction in the game, wielding immense power, with a harsh yet
chaotic discipline.*
*Culture: Employees show little pity for each other, even mocking colleagues' deaths. The company
provides room and board, and maintains facilities like a boot camp, training gym, cafeteria, and
underground fighting arena.*
*Management: Supervisors can arbitrarily punish or even execute subordinates. Giant trash bins in
the corridors are suspected of being used for employee corpse disposal.*
*Identification: The only identifier for an employee is a number printed on the sole of their long
socks, which also serves as the bounty voucher (Lubi) for defeating them.*

### 2.2 员工等级与兵种 / Employee Ranks & Types

兽盾员工根据制服颜色和职责分为多个等级：
BeastShield employees are ranked by uniform color and duty:

- **G级 - 杂兵 (Grunt)**：最低阶，绿色制服。负责巡逻、看守等基础体力工作。数量最多，常被用作“炮灰”。赏金100撸币。
- **O级 - 监工 (Overseer)**：低级干部，蓝色制服。负责监督和管理G级员工。赏金200撸币。
- **E级 - 武斗兵 (Enforcer)**：高级近战兵种，黑色制服，配备护裆。负责镇压、守卫、押运等强力任务。赏金500撸币。
- **R级 - 枪械兵 (Ranged)**：高级远程兵种，白色制服。负责火力支援与狙击。赏金500撸币。
- **W级 - 摔跤兵 (Wrestler)**：精英单位，深蓝色连体摔跤服。负责角斗表演与极端武力处决。赏金1000撸币。

*G-Level - Grunt: Lowest rank, green uniform. Handles basic patrols, guard duty. Highest in number,
often used as cannon fodder. Bounty: 100 Lubi.*
*O-Level - Overseer: Junior supervisor, blue uniform. Oversees and manages G-Level grunts. Bounty:
200 Lubi.*
*E-Level - Enforcer: Advanced melee unit, black uniform with groin guard. Handles suppression,
guarding, escorting. Bounty: 500 Lubi.*
*R-Level - Ranged: Advanced ranged unit, white uniform. Provides fire support and sniping. Bounty:
500 Lubi.*
*W-Level - Wrestler: Elite unit, dark blue wrestling singlet. Performs in fights and extreme
enforcement. Bounty: 1000 Lubi.*

### 2.3 游戏核心机制 / Core Game Mechanics

- **性欲值系统 (Libido System)**：所有伤害转化为目标的性欲值，蓄满时目标射精死亡。角色拥有可视的“性欲槽”。
- **成长系统 (Progression System)**：通过处决敌人获得经验升级，分配属性点至不同成长系（战斗、射击、潜行、英雄）。
- **特殊系统 (Special Systems)**：
    - **随从系统 (Companion System)**：可收服一名兽人作为永久随从。
    - **换装技能 (Disguise Skill)**：穿上敌人的全套制服可获得强力伪装效果。
    - **隐藏资料 (Hidden Profiles)**：通过特定方式刺激兽人可解锁查看其详细属性与习性。

*Libido System: All damage is converted into the target's libido. Upon reaching the limit, the
target ejaculates to death. Characters have a visible "Libido Gauge."*
*Progression System: Gain XP by executing enemies, level up, and allocate attribute points to
different specializations (Combat, Shooting, Stealth, Hero).*
*Special Systems:

- Companion System: Can permanently recruit one beastman as a follower.
- Disguise Skill: Wearing an enemy's full uniform grants a powerful disguise effect.
- Hidden Profiles: Stimulating a beastman in specific ways unlocks their detailed stats and traits.*

## 3. 世界与地点 / World & Locations

### 3.1 主要区域

- **初始仓库 (The Starting Warehouse)**：教学关卡与故事起点。
- **荒野与道路 (The Wilderness & Road)**：连接仓库与市中心的过渡地带，设有运货卡车等交通工具。
- **白之都 (The White City)**：游戏的主要城市，高楼林立，繁华喧闹。设有武器店/赏金工会等关键地点。
- **兽盾总部大厦 (BeastShield Headquarters)**：位于白之都中心，高达数十层。守卫实力随楼层递增，董事长“牙”的办公室位于顶层。
- **地下格斗场 (Underground Fighting Arena)**：兽盾运营的生死角斗场所。

*The Starting Warehouse: The tutorial area and story's beginning.*
*The Wilderness & Road: Transitional area connecting the warehouse to downtown, featuring transport
like cargo trucks.*
*The White City: The game's main urban center, a bustling metropolis with skyscrapers. Key locations
include weapon shops/bounty guilds.*
*BeastShield Headquarters: Located downtown, a skyscraper dozens of stories high. Guard strength
increases per floor. Chairman "Ya"'s office is at the top.*
*Underground Fighting Arena: A Beast Shield-operated deathmatch arena.*

## 4. 关键角色与种族 / Key Characters & Races

### 4.1 玩家角色

一名意外进入“兽游世界”的玩家。初始身份可能是被剿灭团伙的幸存者。性格谨慎，倾向于潜行玩法，在游戏中经历从挣扎求生到逐步变强，甚至渗入兽盾内部的过程。

*A player who accidentally enters the "Beast Game World." Initial identity might be a survivor of a
wiped-out gang. Cautious by nature, favors stealth gameplay. Experiences growth from struggling to
survive to becoming powerful, even infiltrating BeastShield.*

### 4.2 兽人种族特征

- **牛兽人**：力量强大，储精量惊人，生命力顽强，易发情。可能拥有“憋精功”等保命技能。
- **熊兽人**：体型肥壮或膀大腰圆，性格可能憨厚。部分个体掌握“金钟罩”技能。
- **虎兽人**：常担任队长等职务，实力强劲，性格冷酷。高级兵种（如虎队长）是重要对手。
- **狼兽人/狗兽人**：外观多样，敏感点明确，是常见的杂兵或中级兵种。
- **其他种族**：包括野猪、狮子、豹、犀牛等，各具特色。

*Bovine Beastmen: High strength, incredible semen reserves, tenacious vitality, prone to arousal.
May possess survival skills like "Ejaculation Suppression Technique."*
*Ursine Beastmen: Large, burly, or portly. Can be simple and honest. Some know the "Iron Shirt"
skill.*
*Tiger Beastmen: Often serve as captains or leaders, powerful and冷酷. Advanced units (e.g., Tiger
Captain) are major opponents.*
*Wolf/Canine Beastmen: Varied appearances, clear sensitive spots. Common as grunts or mid-tier
soldiers.*
*Other Races: Include boars, lions, leopards, rhinos, etc., each with distinct traits.*

### 4.3 平民与其它势力

- **白熊兽人（武道馆馆长）**：富有正义感的强大平民，会出手“替天行道”。
- **小熊猫兽人（武器店主）**：可爱的小型兽人，经营武器店兼赏金工会接发点。
- **警察系统**：名义上维持秩序，但慑于兽盾权势，对其恶行往往睁一只眼闭一只眼。
- **特工局**：后期出现的隐秘组织，旨在对抗兽盾，其成员实力深不可测。

*White Bear Beastmen (Dojo Master): A powerful civilian with a strong sense of justice, willing to "
deliver heavenly justice."*
*Red Panda Beastmen (Weapon Shop Owner): A cute, small beastman who runs a weapon shop that also
serves as a bounty guild contact.*
*Police Force: Nominally maintains order but often turns a blind eye to BeastShield's actions due to
fear of their power.*
*Special Agent Bureau: A covert organization appearing later, aiming to counter BeastShield, with
members of unfathomable strength.*

## 5. 物品与经济 / Items & Economy

- **货币**：撸币 (Lubi)，通过提交兽盾员工袜底的编号兑换。
- **武器**：从低级的“突刺匕首”、“钢丝指环”到高级的“精钢匕首”、“麻痹戒指”等。
- **道具**：如“催眠瓦斯弹”，可使范围内兽人昏睡。
- **赏金系统**：击杀兽盾员工后，需取其一只带编号的袜子前往武器店兑换赏金。成为“高级赏金猎人”后可自动结算已击杀过的兵种。

*Currency: Lubi, obtained by trading in the numbered socks of defeated BeastShield employees.*
*Weapons: Range from low-tier "Stabbing Dagger," "Wire Garrote" to high-tier "Fine Steel Dagger," "
Paralysis Ring," etc.*
*Items: Such as "Hypnotic Gas Grenade," which can put beastmen in an area to sleep.*
*Bounty System: After killing a BeastShield employee, take one of their numbered socks to a weapon
shop to claim the bounty. "Advanced Bounty Hunters" get automatic rewards for previously defeated
unit types.*

## 6. 故事概要（基于主线上篇） / Story Synopsis (Based on Main Story Part 1)

玩家从仓库角落醒来，通过“性欲值”机制艰难完成教学关卡。在逃离仓库、前往市中心后，开始接触并了解兽盾公司的恶行。为了生存和变强，玩家开始狩猎兽盾员工以换取赏金，并学会了“换装”等技能。

一次偶然，玩家被卷入兽盾公司的行动，并因误打误撞“立功”而晋升为O级监工，获得了下属牛杂和熊掌。在经历转职任务、挑战本部大楼、与高级兵种周旋后，玩家最终被兽盾董事长“牙”发现并植入“血精石”，变异为“嗜精兽”。在被迫加入对抗兽盾的“特工局”并戴上封印神器“青雉缚阳锁”后，玩家的命运迎来了新的转折……

*The player awakens in a warehouse corner, barely completing the tutorial using the "Libido"
mechanic. After escaping the warehouse and reaching downtown, they learn of the BeastShield
Corporation's evils. To survive and grow stronger, the player starts hunting BeastShield employees
for bounties and learns skills like "Disguise."*

*By chance, the player gets involved in a BeastShield operation and, through a misunderstanding,
is "rewarded" with a promotion to O-Level Overseer, gaining subordinates Niu Za and Xiong Zhang.
After undergoing a class-change quest, challenging the headquarters, and confronting advanced units,
the player is eventually discovered by Chairman "Ya" and implanted with a "Blood Essence Stone,"
mutating into a "Semen-Devouring Beast." Forced to join the BeastShield-opposing "Special Agent
Bureau" and equipped with the sealing artifact "Azure Pheasant Yang-Binding Lock," the player's fate
takes a new turn...*
