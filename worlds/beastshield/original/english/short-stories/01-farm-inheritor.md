**Creative Background:**
This story introduces the core setting of **semen as a supernatural agricultural resource** in the
BeastShield Universe, demonstrating how ordinary individuals can use the physiological resources of
BeastShield Corporation employees to fight back. Through the unique perspective of "hostile resource
conversion and utilization," the story materializes the class struggle in the BeastShield Universe
as an absurd yet effective survival strategy. It provides a non-traditional yet highly creative
solution for **individual resistance against the BeastShield Corporation** throughout the series.
This "turning their own weapons against them" concept reflects the survival wisdom of underdog
characters while deepening the themes of resource contention and power reversal in the BeastShield
Universe.
