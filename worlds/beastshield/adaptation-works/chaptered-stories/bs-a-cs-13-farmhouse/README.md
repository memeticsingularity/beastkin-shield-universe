## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：社畜牛兽人小谷继承爷爷的实验农场，以兽盾员工为"肥料"种植神奇作物，在复仇与经营之间逐渐建立起属于自己的黑暗产业帝国。

社畜牛兽人**小谷**在事业低谷时继承了爷爷留下的实验农场。这片农场种植的不是普通作物，而是需要兽人精液灌溉的"蛮力萝卜"、"欲望黑参"、"活力紫茎"等特殊植物。为了获取肥料，小谷将目标对准了隔壁的兽盾分公司——那些曾欺辱过他的兽盾员工。他开设"兽盾农家乐"餐厅，在菜中下迷药，将送上门来的兽盾员工一一捕获、榨精、转化为"肥料"或跟班。

随着规模扩大，小谷逐渐从单纯的复仇快意转向经营野心，手下跟班之间复杂的忠诚与裂痕让这条黑暗道路愈发动荡不安。然而，当一个又一个曾经的"敌人"变成"自己人"，当德牧警官雷克斯意外闯入这个畸形的大家庭，农家乐的性质也在悄然改变——从黑暗的复仇工具，逐渐变成一个温暖而离谱的奇特家庭。

**One-line Hook**: A down-on-his-luck bull beastkin inherits an experimental farm that grows magical crops fertilized by beastman semen, and builds a dark empire by harvesting his tormentors from the neighboring Beastshield branch.

*Beastshield Farmhouse* follows **Xiao Gu**, a struggling bull beastkin who inherits his grandfather's experimental farm. The farm grows extraordinary plants like "Brute Radishes," "Lust Black Ginseng," and "Vitality Purple Stem"—all requiring beastman semen as fertilizer. To obtain this resource, Xiao Gu opens the "Beastshield Farmhouse" restaurant next to a Beastshield branch, drugging their employees and converting them into "fertilizer" or subordinates.

As his operation grows, his motivations shift from pure revenge to genuine ambition, while the complex loyalties and fractures among his recruits make his dark path increasingly volatile. Yet, as one former "enemy" after another becomes "family," and when police officer Rex unexpectedly stumbles into this twisted household, the farmhouse begins to transform—from a tool of dark vengeance into a warm, absurd, and unconventional family.
