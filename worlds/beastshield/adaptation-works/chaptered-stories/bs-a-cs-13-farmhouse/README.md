## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：社畜牛兽人小谷继承爷爷的实验农场，以兽盾员工为"肥料"种植神奇作物，在复仇与经营之间逐渐建立起属于自己的黑暗产业帝国。

社畜牛兽人**小谷**在事业低谷时继承了爷爷留下的实验农场。这片农场种植的不是普通作物，而是需要兽人精液灌溉的"蛮力萝卜"、"欲望黑参"等特殊植物。为了获取肥料，小谷将目标对准了隔壁的兽盾分公司——那些曾欺辱过他的兽盾员工。他开设"兽盾农家乐"餐厅，在菜中下迷药，将送上门来的兽盾员工一一捕获、榨精、转化为"肥料"或跟班。随着规模扩大，小谷逐渐从单纯的复仇快意转向经营野心，而手下跟班之间复杂的忠诚与裂痕，也让这条黑暗道路愈发动荡不安。

---

**One-line Hook**: A down-on-his-luck bull beastkin inherits an experimental farm that grows magical crops fertilized by beastman semen, and builds a dark empire by harvesting his tormentors from the neighboring Beastshield branch.

*Beastshield Farmhouse* follows **Xiao Gu**, a struggling bull beastkin who inherits his grandfather's experimental farm. The farm grows extraordinary plants like "Brute Radishes" and "Lust Black Ginseng"—all requiring beastman semen as fertilizer. To obtain this resource, Xiao Gu opens the "Beastshield Farmhouse" restaurant next to a Beastshield branch, drugging their employees and converting them into "fertilizer" or subordinates. As his operation grows, his motivations shift from pure revenge to genuine ambition, while the complex loyalties and fractures among his recruits make his dark path increasingly volatile.
