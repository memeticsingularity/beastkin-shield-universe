## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：当一位"全掌控型玩家"带着满级外挂降临兽游世界，仓库里的每一只兽人都成了待收集的战利品。

《All Clear》是《兽盾》世界观下的一部同人衍生章节化故事，聚焦于"全杀路线 + 多系玩法 + 开局满技能"的高自由度叙事体验。故事围绕一位拥有"全掌控型玩家"心智的主角展开，他通过外挂能力在《兽游世界》中系统性地清除兽盾公司员工，并收集其衣物、袜子、纪念品等"战利品"，体验不同战斗风格带来的差异化叙事。本故事深度融合系统提示、存档机制、角色状态等元叙事元素，强调冷峻、高效、收集导向的游戏化叙事。

---

**One-line Hook**: When an omnipotent controller player with max-level cheats descends into the Beastgame World, every beastkin in the warehouse becomes a collectible trophy.

*All Clear* is a fan-made chaptered story set within the *Beastshield* universe, focusing on a high-freedom narrative experience characterized by an "all-kill route + multi-style gameplay + full skills from the start." The story revolves around a protagonist with the mindset of an "omnipotent controller player," who utilizes cheat abilities to systematically eliminate Beastshield Company employees and collect their clothing, socks, memorabilia, and other "trophies." It deeply integrates meta-narrative elements such as system prompts, save mechanics, and character status.
