## 1. 项目简介 / Project Introduction

**中文简介**
《All Clear》是《兽盾》世界观下的一部同人衍生章节化故事，聚焦于"全杀路线 + 多系玩法 + 开局满技能"
的高自由度叙事体验。故事围绕一位拥有"全掌控型玩家"
心智的主角展开，他通过外挂能力（满级、自由加点、无限存档）在《兽游世界》中系统性地清除兽盾公司员工，并收集其衣物、袜子、纪念品等"
战利品"，体验不同战斗风格带来的差异化叙事。

本故事与《Beastshield Paradise》温暖治愈的走向形成鲜明对比，强调冷峻、高效、收集导向的游戏化叙事，并深度融合系统提示、存档机制、角色状态等元叙事元素。

---

**English Introduction**
*All Clear* is a fan-made chaptered story set within the *Beastshield* universe, focusing on a
high-freedom narrative experience characterized by an "all-kill route + multi-style gameplay + full
skills from the start." The story revolves around a protagonist with the mindset of an "omnipotent
controller player," who utilizes cheat abilities (max level, free attribute allocation, infinite
saves) to systematically eliminate Beastshield Company employees and collect their clothing, socks,
memorabilia, and other "trophies" within the *Beastgame World*. This allows for experiencing
different combat styles and their corresponding narrative variations.

This story stands in stark contrast to the warm, healing direction of *Beastshield Paradise*,
emphasizing a cold, efficient, collection-oriented gamified narrative. It deeply integrates
meta-narrative elements such as system prompts, save mechanics, and character status.

---

## 2. 核心特色 / Core Features

**中文特色**

- **全杀路线原则**：每个遭遇必须击杀目标，战后存档只记录死亡状态，无逃脱或饶恕选项
- **多系玩法叙事**：战斗、射击、潜行、英雄四大系自由切换，叙事风格随之变化
- **开局满技能设定**：主角初始即拥有"换装"与"影袭"技能，增强战术多样性
- **自动存档机制**：战前与战后自动触发存档，叙事与系统深度融合
- **战利品收集系统**：袜子、制服、纪念品等作为资产被系统化收集与记录
- **元叙事元素**：系统界面、状态提示、资产列表直接融入叙事文本
- **冷峻客观风格**：叙事语调保持冷静客观，强调机制与效率

---

**English Features**

- **All-Kill Route Principle**: Every encounter must end with the target's death. Post-battle saves
  only record deceased states, with no escape or mercy options.
- **Multi-Style Narrative**: Free switching between Combat, Shooting, Stealth, and Hero styles, with
  narrative tone adapting accordingly.
- **Full Skills from Start**: The protagonist begins with the "Disguise" and "Shadow Assault"
  skills, enhancing tactical diversity.
- **Auto-Save Mechanism**: Automatic save triggers before and after encounters, deeply integrating
  narrative with system mechanics.
- **Trophy Collection System**: Socks, uniforms, memorabilia, etc., are systematically collected and
  recorded as assets.
- **Meta-Narrative Elements**: System interfaces, status prompts, and asset lists are directly
  integrated into the narrative text.
- **Cold, Objective Style**: The narrative tone remains冷静 and objective, emphasizing mechanics and
  efficiency.

## 4. 叙事机制详解 / Narrative Mechanics Explained

### 4.1 自动存档系统 / Auto-Save System

**中文机制**

- **战前存档**：当主角确认目标并准备行动时自动触发，记录场景与目标状态
- **战后存档**：主角完成袜子收集（脱下袜子）后自动触发，更新资产列表
- **存档编号**：格式为`SAVE-XXXXXX`，按时间顺序从`SAVE-000000`开始递增
- **场景快照**：每次存档包含当前环境的详细描述，定格叙事瞬间
- **资产记录**：袜子、武器、服装、纪念品被系统化分类记录

---

**English Mechanics**

- **Pre-Battle Save**: Automatically triggers when the protagonist identifies a target and prepares
  to act, recording scene and target status.
- **Post-Battle Save**: Automatically triggers after the protagonist completes sock collection (
  removing socks), updating the asset list.
- **Save Numbering**: Format `SAVE-XXXXXX`, incrementing sequentially from `SAVE-000000`.
- **Scene Snapshot**: Each save includes a detailed description of the current environment, freezing
  the narrative moment.
- **Asset Logging**: Socks, weapons, clothing, and memorabilia are systematically categorized and
  recorded.

### 4.2 四大系叙事差异 / Four-Style Narrative Differences

**中文差异**

- **英雄系 (Hero)**: 强调互动、挑衅、受制，可触发特殊战败路线，叙事带有沉浸式体验感
- **潜行系 (Stealth)**: 强调隐蔽、感知、一击制敌，叙事冷峻精准，注重环境细节
- **战斗系 (Combat)**: 强调正面碾压、力量对抗，叙事高效冷酷，注重动作描写
- **射击系 (Shooting)**: 强调远程狙击、视野控制，叙事专注冷静，注重空间关系

---

**English Differences**

- **Hero Style**: Emphasizes interaction, provocation, and being subdued. Can trigger special defeat
  routes. Narrative has an immersive experience quality.
- **Stealth Style**: Emphasizes concealment, perception, and one-hit takedowns. Narrative is冷峻 and
  precise, focusing on environmental details.
- **Combat Style**: Emphasizes frontal domination and force对抗. Narrative is efficient and冷酷,
  focusing on action descriptions.
- **Shooting Style**: Emphasizes long-range sniping and视野 control. Narrative is focused and calm,
  emphasizing spatial relationships.

### 4.3 资产系统 / Asset System

**中文资产类型**

1. **袜子 (双)**: 核心战利品，脱下即触发战后存档，按角色编号分类收集
2. **武器**: 可装备的战斗工具，具有功能性描述与编号标识
3. **服装**: 穿戴的衣物护具，部分具备特殊效果（如"换装"伪装）
4. **纪念品**: 叙事性物品，记录角色背景与世界观碎片，无功能性

---

**English Asset Types**

1. **Socks (Pair)**: Core trophies. Removing them triggers post-battle saves. Collected and
   categorized by character number.
2. **Weapons**: Equippable combat tools with functional descriptions and numbering.
3. **Clothing**: Wearable garments and armor. Some have special effects (e.g., "Disguise"
   camouflage).
4. **Memorabilia**: Narrative items that record character backgrounds and world-building碎片.
   Non-functional.

### 4.4 技能系统 / Skill System

**中文技能详解**

- **换装 (Disguise)**: 穿上目标制服后可完美伪装成该角色外貌体型，对电子监控与一般认知有效
- **影袭 (Shadow Assault)**: 瞬间移动至锁定的敌人或目标阴影处，移动过程无痕迹声响，冷却极短

---

**English Skill Details**

- **Disguise**: After putting on a target's uniform, the wearer perfectly mimics the target's
  appearance and physique. Effective against electronic surveillance and general perception.
- **Shadow Assault**: Instantly teleports to the shadow of a locked-on enemy or target. The movement
  leaves几乎 no trace or sound. Very short cooldown.

## 8. 创作理念 / Creative Philosophy

**中文创作理念**
本故事试图探索以下叙事可能性：

- **游戏机制与叙事的深度融合**：将存档、状态、资产等系统元素直接融入叙事文本
- **外挂玩家心理在叙事中的体现**：通过主角行为展现"全掌控型玩家"的思维方式与决策逻辑
- **"全杀"路线下的角色塑造**：在必须击杀的前提下，通过对话、求饶、背景碎片塑造有血有肉的目标
- **多系玩法对同一事件的不同叙事**：同一遭遇因玩法选择而产生完全不同的叙事体验与细节描写
- **冷峻风格的情感张力**：在客观冷静的叙述中，通过细节与环境烘托情感冲击

---

**English Creative Philosophy**
This story attempts to explore the following narrative possibilities:

- **Deep Integration of Game Mechanics and Narrative**: Directly incorporating system elements like
  saves, status, and assets into the narrative text.
- **Manifestation of Cheater Player Psychology in Narrative**: Demonstrating the thought processes
  and decision logic of the "Omnipotent Controller Player" through the protagonist's actions.
- **Character Development under the "All-Kill" Route**: Creating fleshed-out targets through
  dialogue, pleas, and background fragments, within the constraint of necessary elimination.
- **Different Narratives for the Same Event via Multiple Playstyles**: Completely different
  narrative experiences and detailed descriptions for the same encounter based on gameplay choices.
- **Emotional Tension in a Cold Style**: Using details and environment to烘托 emotional impact
  within an objective,冷静叙述.
