## Introduction

*Daily* is a branch chapter series in the *Beastshield* universe, focusing on the everyday life slices of ordinary Beastshield soldiers and civilians. It does not pursue grand narratives, but rather presents the survival details, emotional bonds, and small acts of resistance of individuals beneath the iron curtain through a group perspective.

## Content Features

- **Multi-character POV**: Each chapter unfolds centered on a different character
- **Sensory realism**: Emphasizes smell, touch, environmental details
- **De-heroization**: Protagonists are ordinary soldiers, interns, bottom-level clerks
