## 简介

赏金猎人**炎**与**魉**的死亡线故事。两人潜入兽盾新兵训练基地，一个负责杀戮，一个负责善后，在血腥与欲望中揭示生命与死亡的边界。

---

## I. Introduction

The death-line story of bounty hunters **Yan** and **Liang**. Infiltrating a Beastshield recruit training base—one kills, one cleans up—they reveal the boundary between life and death through blood and desire.
