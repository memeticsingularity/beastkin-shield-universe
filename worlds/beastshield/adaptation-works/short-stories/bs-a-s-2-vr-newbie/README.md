## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：当他第一次戴上VR头盔，他以为这只是一款普通的游戏。但当那温热腥臊的液体溅到他脸上时，他才明白——这不是游戏，这是另一个真实的世界。

程序员**陈默**花三个月工资买了沉浸式VR套件《元兽御主》，以为只是一场游戏。当他以棕熊兽人的身躯站在这个世界的第一条街道上，第一次目睹真实的死亡、第一次感受鲜血溅在脸上的温度、第一次跪在路边呕吐时，他才明白：对土生土长的"NPC"而言，这就是他们唯一真实的生活。而他的第一场游戏，才刚刚开始。

---

**One-line Hook**: A VR player thinks he's booting up a game—until warm blood splatters across his fur and he realizes the "NPCs" are living, dying, and hoping just like him.

Chen Mo, a programmer, spends three months' salary on an immersive VR kit for *Meta Beast Master*, expecting just another game. When he stands on a street in the body of a brown bear, witnesses real death for the first time, feels blood spray across his face, and vomits by the roadside, he understands: for the native "NPCs," this is their only real life. And his first game has only just begun.
