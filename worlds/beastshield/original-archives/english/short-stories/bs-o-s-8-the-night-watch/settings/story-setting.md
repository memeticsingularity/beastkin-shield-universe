# SS-008: The Night Watch - Story-Specific Settings

---

## Story-Specific Characters

### Shiya (G-612)

**Basic Information**

- **Species**: Wild boar beastman
- **Code**: G-612
- **Class**: G-level Grunt
- **Age**: 28
- **Height**: 170cm
- **Build**: Muscle wrapped in fat, body width over one meter

**Appearance**

- Black-brown fur
- Prominent tusks (cloudy yellow)
- Full hermaphroditic chest muscles
- Round belly
- Short but sturdy pig dick
- Wearing yellowed white long socks (code G-612)

**Personality**

- Honest and dutiful, no ambition
- Filial to parents, wants to earn money to support family
- Resigned to fate, doesn't complain
- Tolerant of companions (e.g., Xiong Dabao's laziness)

**Backstory**
From a remote wild boar village, parents are both farmers. Has worked at Beastshield for three years, saving money to build a house in his hometown. Dreams of opening a small grocery store to live a stable life.

**Ending**
Killed by bounty hunter Shadowclaw while keeping watch, begged "I still have parents to support" before dying.

---

### Xiong Dabao (G-611)

**Basic Information**

- **Species**: Brown bear beastman
- **Code**: G-611
- **Class**: G-level Grunt

**Appearance**

- Height nearly two meters, weight over 300 jin
- Dark brown dense fur
- Specially made oversized military green uniform
- Rich bear-specific body odor

**Personality**

- Lazy, would rather lie down than sit
- Pushes night shifts onto Shiya
- But not bad-natured, occasionally shares snacks

**Backstory**
Dreams of saving enough money to return home and open a honey shop.

**Ending**
Shadowclaw's throwing knife inserted into the back of his neck, instant death.

---

### Shadowclaw

**Basic Information**

- **Species**: Gray wolf beastman
- **Occupation**: Bounty hunter
- **Code**: Shadowclaw (not real name)
- **Years in profession**: 5

**Appearance**

- Lean build, smooth muscle lines
- Gray-black fur (can hide in night)
- Green eyes
- Long, powerful hands covered in calluses

**Personality**

- Professional, calm, numb
- Long accustomed to killing, indifferent to life
- But Shiya's last words caused a slight waver
- Began to doubt his profession

**Backstory**
Has worked in the bounty hunter industry for five years, killed over fifty Beastshield employees. Has no other skills, can only make a living through killing.

## Themes and Symbols

### Core Themes

1. **Tragedy of the small character**: Ordinary people just want to live peacefully, but are crushed by the world
2. **Numbness and wavering**: A killer experiences a ripple of humanity because of his prey's words
3. **Family and dreams**: Death takes away unfulfilled promises

### Symbols

- **Yellowed numbered socks**: Forgotten identity, worn-down existence
- **Osmanthus cake and cotton-padded jacket**: Unredeemed promises
- **Night watch**: Waiting for a dawn that will never come
