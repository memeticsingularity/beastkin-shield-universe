# SS-008: The Night Watch / 守夜人

---

## Story Synopsis

In a remote **Beastshield warehouse**, a **wild boar beastman guard** is keeping watch alone. He thinks this is just another one of countless boring nights, never knowing that Death has already donned the cloak of night and is quietly approaching.

This is a story about the tragic fate of an ordinary small character, showcasing the survival predicament of bottom-level employees in the **Beastshield** world, and the complex relationship between bounty hunters and their prey.
