## Synopsis / 故事简介

**Yan** and **Liang** are a bounty hunter duo specializing in hunting Beastshield Company guards for rewards. Yan excels at quickly subduing targets with Scorching Flow Dark Force, possessing a cold and efficient personality; Liang has a chameleon's innate invisibility talent, responsible for cleanup and recording sock numbers, with a gentle and kind nature. The two have contrasting personalities yet work in perfect harmony, launching a thrilling hunting operation at the Beastshield recruit training base.

**炎**与**魉**是一对赏金猎人搭档，专门狩猎兽盾公司的守卫以换取赏金。炎擅长以灼流暗劲快速制服目标，性格冷酷高效；魉拥有变色龙先天隐身天赋，负责善后与记录编号，性格温和善良。两人性格迥异却配合默契，在兽盾新兵训练基地展开了一场惊心动魄的猎杀行动。

## Core Themes / 核心主题

- **Contrast of Coldness and Mercy / 冷酷与怜悯的对比**: Yan's ruthless executions contrast sharply with Liang's humane concern / 炎的无情处决与魉的人道关怀形成鲜明对照
- **Bounty Hunter's Code / 赏金猎人的生存法则**: The moral dilemma of making a living through hunting in a cruel world / 在残酷世界中以狩猎为生的道德困境
- **Darkness of the Beastshield System / 兽盾体制的黑暗**: From uniform reform to minister-level dereliction punishment, showcasing the organization's cruelty / 从制服改革到部长级失职惩罚，展现组织的冷酷
- **Overwhelming Power / 强者碾压**: Panda's intervention demonstrates the gap between true top-tier powerhouses and ordinary guards / 熊猫的出手展示了真正顶尖强者与普通守卫之间的鸿沟
