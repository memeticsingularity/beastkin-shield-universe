## 2. Story Overview

A furry enthusiast acquires a mysterious VR game called *Beast World*. In the game, all damage and
attacks are converted into "Lust Value." When Lust Value reaches its limit, the character will "
ejaculate" to death. Players must utilize this unique mechanism to survive, grow, and gradually
uncover the secrets of the Beastshield Company.

This volume is the first part of the overarching story, consisting of 13 chapters. It follows the
player from entering the game, experiencing the tutorial, initially exploring Beastshield
headquarters, encountering the Agent Bureau, to finally uncovering the secret of the Blood Essence
Stone within their body.

## 4. Worldview Setting (Mentioned in This Volume)

### 4.1 Major Organizations

- **Beastshield Company**: The most powerful organization in White Capital, nearly rivaling the
  government, involved in arms smuggling, contraband trafficking, etc.
- **Agent Bureau**: A secret organization opposing Beastshield, with its own base and members.
- **Martial Arts Dojo**: A civilian martial arts organization; the Bear Dojo Master is a member.

### 4.2 Beastshield Company Rank System

1. **Ordinary Employee/Grunt**: Green uniform.
2. **Cadre**: Blue uniform (obtained by the protagonist after promotion).
3. **Combat Soldier**: Black uniform (Tiger Captain's unit).
4. **Wrestler Soldier**: Dark blue wrestling singlet (trained in underground fighting rings).
5. **Advanced Classes**: Including wrestler soldiers, firearms soldiers (snipers), etc.; uniform
   colors not consistently mentioned.

### 4.3 Game Mechanics

1. **Semen Gauge System**: All damage is converted into the Semen Gauge (Lust Value). Full gauge
   results in ejaculatory death.
2. **Attribute System**: Four growth paths: Combat, Shooting, Stealth, Heroic.
3. **Skill System**: Includes skills like Apply Poison, Disguise, Shadow Assault, Silver Needle
   Sealing.
4. **Follower System**: Can recruit beastkin as followers. Initial follower slot is one.
5. **Bounty System**: Killing Beastshield employees yields bounties, redeemable via sock numbers.

### 4.4 Special Settings

1. **Blood Essence Stone**: A red crystal that, when implanted, mutates the host into a "Semen-Eater
   Beast."
2. **Semen-Eater Beast**: An aberration that must continuously consume beastkin semen to survive.
3. **Flowing Ruin**: A black slime monster that consumes semen, a bioweapon created by Beastshield.
4. **Azure Sparrow Yang-Binding Lock**: One of the Twelve Divine Weapons, capable of sealing all
   abnormal states within the body.
