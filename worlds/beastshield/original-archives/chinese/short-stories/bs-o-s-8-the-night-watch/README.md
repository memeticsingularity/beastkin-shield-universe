## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：偏僻的兽盾仓库里，一只G级杂兵以为这只是无数个无聊夜晚中的一个，却不知道死神已经披上了夜色的外衣。

偏僻的兽盾仓库里，一只野猪兽人守卫正在独自守夜。他以为这只是无数个无聊夜晚中的一个，却不知道死神已经披上了夜色的外衣，正悄悄向他走来。这是一个关于平凡小人物悲剧命运的故事，展现了兽盾世界底层员工的生存困境，以及赏金猎人与猎物之间复杂的关系。

---

**One-line Hook**: In a remote Beastshield warehouse, a G-level grunt thinks this is just another boring night shift — unaware that Death has already donned the cloak of darkness.

In a remote Beastshield warehouse, a boar beastkin guard is keeping watch alone. He assumes this is just another of countless dull nights, never realizing that Death has already wrapped itself in the garment of night and is creeping toward him. This is a story about the tragic fate of an ordinary small-timer, revealing the survival predicament of bottom-rung employees in the Beastshield world, and the complex relationship between bounty hunter and prey.
