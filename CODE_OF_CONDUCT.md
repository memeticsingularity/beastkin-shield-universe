# Beastkin Universe 社区行为准则

Code of Conduct for Beastkin Universe Community

---

## 1. 我们的承诺

## 1. Our Pledge

我们作为项目的贡献者和维护者，承诺营造一个对所有人开放、友好、尊重、安全的创作环境，不论其年龄、体型、残疾、种族、性别认同与表达、经验水平、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教信仰或性取向如何。  
We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

我们承诺以促进开放、包容、多元和健康社区的方式行事和互动。  
We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

---

## 2. 行为标准

## 2. Standards of Conduct

### 2.1 积极行为

### 2.1 Positive Behaviors

以下行为有助于营造积极的社区环境：  
The following behaviors are expected and encouraged:

- **尊重与包容**：尊重不同的观点、创作风格和文化背景。
- **Respect and Inclusion**: Respect different viewpoints, creative styles, and cultural
  backgrounds.

- **建设性反馈**：提供具体、有帮助的反馈，聚焦于作品本身而非个人。
- **Constructive Feedback**: Provide specific, helpful feedback focused on the work, not the person.

- **创作诚信**：尊重原作者的设定和创作意图，明确标注改编和原创内容。
- **Creative Integrity**: Respect the original author's settings and creative intent, clearly mark
  adaptations and original works.

- **协作精神**：乐于分享知识、帮助新成员、共同完善世界观设定。
- **Collaborative Spirit**: Be willing to share knowledge, help new members, and work together to
  improve world settings.

- **清晰沟通**：以清晰、礼貌的方式进行沟通，尤其是在讨论设定分歧时。
- **Clear Communication**: Communicate clearly and politely, especially when discussing setting
  discrepancies.

### 2.2 不可接受的行为

### 2.2 Unacceptable Behaviors

以下行为被视为不可接受，包括但不限于：  
The following behaviors are considered unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

- **人身攻击**：针对个人的侮辱、贬低、歧视性言论或行为。
- **Personal Attacks**: Insulting, demeaning, discriminatory remarks or behavior directed at an
  individual.

- **骚扰行为**：任何形式的骚扰，包括言语、视觉或身体骚扰。
- **Harassment**: Any form of harassment, including verbal, visual, or physical.

- **恶意破坏**：故意发布错误信息、破坏项目结构或干扰社区正常运行。
- **Malicious Actions**: Deliberately posting false information, damaging project structure, or
  disrupting community operations.

- **版权侵犯**：未经授权使用他人作品，或未按授权要求进行标注。
- **Copyright Infringement**: Using others' works without proper authorization or failing to credit
  as required.

- **设定破坏**：故意违反世界观设定框架，或在未充分讨论的情况下强行引入破坏性的设定变更。
- **Setting Sabotage**: Deliberately violating the world setting framework or forcing disruptive
  setting changes without adequate discussion.

- **垃圾内容**：发布与项目无关的商业广告、垃圾信息或重复内容。
- **Spam**: Posting irrelevant commercial advertisements, spam, or repetitive content.

## 3. 责任与义务

## 3. Responsibilities and Obligations

### 3.1 项目维护者责任

### 3.1 Responsibilities of Project Maintainers

项目维护者有责任：  
Project maintainers are responsible for:

- 明确和执行行为准则。
- Clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

- 对任何不可接受的行为采取适当和公平的纠正措施。
- Taking appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable
  behavior.

- 在适当时删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本行为准则的评论、提交、代码、维基编辑、问题和其他贡献。
- Removing, editing, or rejecting comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other
  contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

- 保护举报人的隐私和安全。
- Protecting the privacy and security of those who report incidents.

### 3.2 贡献者义务

### 3.2 Obligations of Contributors

所有贡献者应：  
All contributors should:

- 熟悉并遵守本行为准则。
- Familiarize themselves with and adhere to this Code of Conduct.

- 协助营造尊重、包容的社区氛围。
- Help create a respectful and inclusive community atmosphere.

- 如遇不可接受的行为，及时向维护者报告。
- Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers in a timely manner.

- 在创作中尊重项目规范，保持设定一致性。
- Respect project norms and maintain setting consistency in their creative works.

## 4. 适用范围

## 4. Scope

本行为准则适用于：  
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- 所有项目空间（GitHub仓库、讨论区、问题追踪等）。
- All project spaces (GitHub repositories, discussion areas, issue trackers, etc.).

- 在公共场合代表项目或社区时（如会议、社交媒体）。
- In public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community (e.g.,
  conferences, social media).

代表项目的行为包括使用官方项目电子邮件地址、通过官方社交媒体账户发布信息，或作为指定代表在线或离线活动。  
Representation of a project may be further defined and clarified by project maintainers.

## 5. 如何报告问题

## 5. Reporting Issues

如果您经历或目睹了不可接受的行为，请通过以下方式报告：  
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

- 发送邮件至：[memeticsingularity@163.com]
- Sending an email to: [memeticsingularity@163.com]

- 在GitHub上向项目维护者发送私信。
- Sending a private message to the project maintainers on GitHub.

所有投诉将被认真、公平地审查和调查。  
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

### 5.1 报告内容

### 5.1 What to Include

请尽可能提供以下信息：  
Please include as much of the following information as possible:

- 事件发生的时间和地点。
- The time and location of the incident.

- 涉及的人员（包括可能的证人）。
- The individuals involved (including any witnesses).

- 事件的具体描述。
- A clear description of what happened.

- 任何相关的上下文信息。
- Any relevant context.

- 您希望的处理方式（如有）。
- How you would like the matter to be addressed (if applicable).

## 6. 处理流程

## 6. Enforcement Process

### 6.1 初步处理

### 6.1 Initial Handling

维护者收到报告后，将：  
Upon receiving a report, maintainers will:

1. 确认收到报告，并告知处理时间线。
1. Acknowledge receipt of the report and provide a timeline for handling.

2. 与相关方沟通，收集更多信息（必要时保护举报人隐私）。
2. Communicate with relevant parties to gather more information (protecting the reporter's privacy
   as needed).

3. 根据行为严重程度，决定处理方式。
3. Decide on an appropriate response based on the severity of the behavior.

### 6.2 处理措施

### 6.2 Enforcement Measures

处理措施可能包括：  
Enforcement measures may include:

- **警告**：针对轻微违规的私下书面警告。
- **Warning**: A private, written warning for minor violations.

- **临时禁言**：在一定时间内禁止参与特定项目活动。
- **Temporary Suspension**: A temporary ban from participating in specific project activities.

- **永久封禁**：对严重或屡次违规者永久禁止参与项目。
- **Permanent Ban**: A permanent ban from participating in the project for severe or repeated
  violations.

所有处理决定将由项目维护者团队集体做出，并尽可能透明地通报（同时保护个人隐私）。  
All enforcement decisions will be made collectively by the project maintainer team and communicated
as transparently as possible (while respecting individual privacy).
